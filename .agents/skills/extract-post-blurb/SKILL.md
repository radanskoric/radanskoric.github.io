Read the post and construct a short blurb to share on social media, RubyFlow, forums and other channels.

Follow these principles:
- Keep it very short: one paragraph, only a few sentences. The very last thing should be the title of the article.
- Keep it light and conversational, avoid overly stiff or corporate sounding language but also don't make it comical.
- Focus on explaining the problem or the question that the article addresses. Only hint at the solution if it makes sense.
- The primary goal is to help the reader to easily understand if the article will be useful to them or not.
- Strongly avoid clickbait!
